About NyaySetu

Bridging the gap between complex Indian laws and everyday citizens through plain-language legal guidance.

Our Mission

NyaySetu (न्यायसेतु) is designed as India's premier Legal First-Response platform. Millions of citizens face legal crises daily—ranging from FIR refusal to workplace harassment—without knowing their rights or instant remedies. We empower citizens with clear, actionable legal advice in plain language.

3

Privacy & Disclaimer

NyaySetu operates strictly with client-side privacy. All user preferences, trusted emergency contacts, and saved articles remain safely stored inside your browser's localStorage. No personal location data or emergency contacts are transmitted to external servers without your explicit action.

Disclaimer: NyaySetu is an informational legal first-response platform, not a legal practice. Information provided here does not constitute formal legal advice.
