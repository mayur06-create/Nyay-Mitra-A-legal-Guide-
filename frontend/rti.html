How to Submit Physically via Speed Post:

  1. Print out 2 copies of this application on A4 paper and sign in the applicant space.
  2. Attach the statutory application fee of ₹10 (via Indian Postal Order, Court Fee Stamp, or Demand Draft payable to the Accounts Officer of the public authority).
  3. Send by Registered Post / Speed Post to the PIO address. Retain the postal tracking receipt as proof of delivery.
  4. Under Section 7(1) of the RTI Act 2005, the public authority is mandated to supply the requested information within 30 days.

PRO TIP

Under Section 6(2) of the RTI Act 2005, an applicant is NOT required to give any reason for requesting the information.
