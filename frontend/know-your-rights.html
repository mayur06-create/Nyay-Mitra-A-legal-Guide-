Know Your Rights

Every citizen should know these key constitutional and legal protections before facing any emergency.

01
Right Against Arbitrary Arrest
Police cannot arrest you without telling you the grounds of arrest immediately. You have the right to consult a lawyer of your choice.
Article 22(1), Constitution of India
02
Right to Free Legal Aid
If you cannot afford a lawyer, the state is legally bound to provide you a qualified legal defense advocate for free through NALSA/DLSA.
Article 39A, Constitution of India
03
Arrest Rules for Women
Women cannot be arrested before sunrise or after sunset, except in exceptional circumstances with prior permission of a Magistrate. Only a female police officer can search a woman.
Sec 46(4), CrPC / BNSS
04
Right to 24-Hour Magistrate Production
Any person arrested by police must be produced before a Magistrate within 24 hours of arrest (excluding travel time).
Sec 57 CrPC & Article 22(2)
05
Protection Against Recovery Agent Harassment
Loan recovery agents cannot call you before 8 AM or after 7 PM, threaten you, or visit your workplace without prior notice.
RBI Fair Practice Code Guidelines
06
Right to Zero FIR
If a cognizable crime takes place, any police station must register a Zero FIR regardless of territorial jurisdiction.
Ministry of Home Affairs Advisory
